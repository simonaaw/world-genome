There is a place for you in this work

Schools, educators, researchers, universities, technology partners, foundations, and volunteers each help bring portable DNA sequencing into classrooms and the field. Find the role that fits you.

Bring the seven-unit curriculum to your classroom and turn your local ecosystem into a living laboratory for your students.

Researchers & universities

04

How it starts

From hello to fieldwork

What happens after you reach out

  1. 1

    We say hello

    A member of our team reads your message and follows up within a few working days to understand your goals and setting.

  2. 2

    We map the ecosystem

    Together we look at the waters, soils, and habitats around you and shape a program that fits your local environment.

  3. 3

    We plan a pilot

    We align on curriculum units, sequencing equipment, training, and timelines, then prepare your cohort for the field.

  4. 4

    We read the living library

    Your students collect, sequence, and analyze eDNA, then add their findings to the shared World Genome Atlas.
